Sally Ride

Intro Today I will tell you about Sally Ride, the first American woman in space.
Becoming a Scientist Sally Ride was born in 1951 and loved science. She studied physics, which is the science of how things work. She worked hard and became a scientist.
Applying to NASA In 1977, NASA was looking for astronauts. For the first time, women could apply. Thousands of people applied, but only a few were chosen. Sally Ride was one of them.
First Woman in Space Becoming an astronaut was not easy. During training, she learned how to live and work in space, where everything floats. In 1983, Sally Ride flew on the space shuttle Challenger and became the first American woman in space. She even returned to space for a second mission.
Investigating Disasters A few years later, some astronauts died when their space shuttles exploded. Because of her knowledge, Sally Ride helped investigate both accidents. Her work helped people understand what went wrong and made space travel safer.
Teaching Kids After leaving NASA, Sally Ride started a program to teach kids about science. Her goal was to make science fun and exciting. She especially wanted girls to know they could be scientists too.
Ending Sally Ride became a role model for many people. She showed that girls can do anything, even go to space.

Blue Crayon

1Dear Duncan, It has been great being your FAVORITE color this past year. And the year before. And the before THAT!
2I have really enjoyed all those oceans, lakes, rivers, raindrops, rain clouds and clear skies.
3But the BAD NEWS is that I am so short and stubby, I can't even see over the railing in the crayon box anymore!
4I need a BREAK! Your very stubby friend, Blue Crayon

Peach Crayon

3How would YOU like to go to school naked? I need some clothes. HELP!
4Your naked friend, Peach Crayon
